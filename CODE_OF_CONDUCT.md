# Community Guidelines

## Our commitment

Participation in Flight_Booking_Web_App_Client should be respectful, constructive, and focused on improving the project. We welcome contributors with different levels of experience and backgrounds.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Give actionable feedback about the work, not personal judgments about contributors.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, or private data.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project and its users.
- Accept that maintainers may reject changes that conflict with project scope, security, or maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, personal attacks, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may close issues or contributions that violate these guidelines. Repeated or severe violations may result in a temporary or permanent restriction from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub's private contact or reporting options. Include relevant links and context. Do not escalate a conduct report by publishing private information.

## Scope

These guidelines apply to repository issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, and other project spaces operated by the maintainers.
